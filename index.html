Mission

Climbing step by step, with method.

Helvetic Dynamics brings together people who love rockets and engineering. We start from high-level model rocketry and work our way towards liquid propulsion.

As on a mountain climb, every stage is prepared: design, calculation, ground testing, then validation in flight. We only move on once the previous stage is mastered.

Programme under way

Ethanol / GOX bi-propellant engine

Ethanol is injected in the centre, oxygen all around it. They mix and burn in a copper chamber protected by a thin film of ethanol, then the gases accelerate through the throat of the nozzle.

Roadmap

From certified rocket to liquid engine.

  1. Stage 1 · Done

    Level 1 certification

    First certified flight with a high-power motor. Design, build and recovery.

  2. Stage 2 · Done

    Level 2 certification

    Higher motor class and written exam. Dual-deployment recovery.

  3. Stage 3 · Under way

    Ethanol / GOX engine

    Sizing of the chamber and nozzle, design of the injector and the feed system.

  4. Stage 4 · Ahead

    Test-stand firings

    Instrumented static firings: chamber pressure, thrust and burn duration.

  5. Stage 5 · Goal

    Liquid-powered flight

    Integration of the engine into a vehicle and first flight on liquid propellants.
